Notes on Harvesting Rainwater in a Small Garden
Published: 3 April 2025

A rain barrel is the cheapest reservoir most households will ever own. Placed under
a downspout, a single barrel fills in one ordinary storm, and that water is softer
and warmer than what comes from the tap, which seedlings prefer. The trick is not
collection but timing: store in the wet weeks, spend in the dry ones.

Mosquitoes are the only real hazard, and a tight lid with a fine screen ends the
argument. A short overflow hose sends the surplus away from the foundation. None of
this is new; it is simply the oldest form of irrigation, remembered.
