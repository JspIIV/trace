Claiming a public page as your own original is cheap and proves nothing. Trace does not try to certify originality: on the open web nobody can prove that from pages they may control. It settles what evidence can: it flags a work as a copy when an earlier, dated source shows it to be one.

register(work_url, title) records a work bound to the caller, starting REGISTERED. challenge(work_id, prior_url) names an earlier page, open to anybody. The contract fetches both and a GenLayer round returns COPY only when the work reproduces the earlier page AND the pages date that page first; the work is then FLAGGED and its author gains a flag. INDEPENDENT and UNCLEAR count the same, as no evidence: nothing clears, no reputation moves, the work stays open. So no strawman self-challenge can lock in a clean record or block later evidence.

Every challenge is kept append-only in the work's history. record(address) is works registered and flagged. Proven on Asimov; tested in tests/trace_rules.py.
