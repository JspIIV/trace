Claiming a public page as your own original is cheap and proves nothing: the same words can be registered by whoever posts them, copied or not.

Trace makes the claim answerable. register(work_url, title) records a work bound to the caller; it starts REGISTERED. challenge(work_id, prior_url), open to anybody, names an earlier page the work is said to be copied from. The contract fetches both pages and a GenLayer round decides COPY, INDEPENDENT or UNCLEAR. COPY flags the registration, INDEPENDENT clears it, and the verdict accrues to the registrant, so record(address) builds an originality track record judged from two public pages.

It never flags on an accusation alone: a challenge runs a round over both pages. If either page cannot be read the verdict is UNCLEAR and the work stays open.

Proven on Asimov with a live register: a work reproducing an earlier essay is COPY and flagged; an independent one is INDEPENDENT and cleared; a challenge against an unreadable page stays UNCLEAR.
